Dashboards & Internal Tools

Your business data, finally clear.

Most small businesses have data scattered across 5+ tools that do not talk to each other. We build custom dashboards and internal tools that pull it all together, surface what matters, and use AI to explain what the numbers mean in plain English.

What we build

We build custom internal tools and dashboards that connect to the systems you already use. The goal is not another tool to manage. It is a single view that makes your existing data actually useful for decision-making.

Reporting command centers

Instead of manually pulling numbers from your CRM, accounting software, and scheduling tool every Monday, a reporting command center pulls it automatically. You see your most important metrics in one place, updated in real time. Weekly KPI summaries are generated as plain-English reports your team can act on without interpretation.

Anomaly detection and alerts

AI monitors your data streams and flags unusual patterns before they become problems. A sudden drop in lead volume, a spike in customer complaints, an unexpected change in revenue per client: the system sends an alert with context so you can respond quickly instead of discovering the issue weeks later in a monthly report.

Plain-English insight summaries

Instead of staring at a spreadsheet trying to figure out what changed, you get a written summary. "Revenue is up 12% week-over-week, driven primarily by 3 new enterprise clients. However, average response time on support tickets increased by 4 hours, which correlates with the 2-point drop in CSAT." This is the kind of insight a good analyst would provide if you could afford one full-time.

Custom internal tools

Sometimes the bottleneck is not analytics but a specific internal process. A pricing calculator that pulls live material costs. A quoting tool that generates proposals from templates. A customer lookup tool that consolidates data from 3 systems into one view. These are small, focused tools that eliminate hours of manual work per week.

How it works

Step 1: We map your data sources

What tools does your business use? CRM, accounting, scheduling, email, spreadsheets, custom databases? We identify where your data lives and how it flows (or does not flow) between systems.

Step 2: We define the metrics that matter

What decisions do you make weekly or daily? What numbers would make those decisions faster or better? We focus on the metrics that drive action, not vanity metrics that look good on a chart but do not change behavior.

Step 3: We build, connect, and deploy

We connect your data sources, build the dashboard or tool, deploy it, and train your team. The result is a tool your team actually uses because it answers real questions and saves real time.

Common integrations

We connect to the tools your business already uses, including:

  • CRM: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, custom databases
  • Accounting: QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks
  • Scheduling: Calendly, Acuity, Google Calendar
  • Communication: Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Microsoft Teams
  • Data sources: Google Sheets, Airtable, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, web scraping
  • Visualization: Recharts, Chart.js, custom interfaces built with React/Next.js

If your data lives somewhere unusual, we can almost certainly connect to it via API, database connection, or file export.

When a dashboard is the right first build

A dashboard is the right starting point when your team spends significant time pulling reports manually, when decisions are delayed because the right data is not accessible, or when you suspect problems but cannot quantify them. It is also a strong foundation: once your data is connected and visible, it becomes much easier to identify which automation to build next.

Common questions about dashboards

How is this different from Google Analytics or my CRM dashboard?

+

Those tools only show data from one system. We pull from multiple sources into a single view and add AI-generated plain-English summaries and anomaly alerts. It is the difference between seeing a number and understanding what it means.

Do we need to change our existing tools?

+

No. The dashboard connects to your existing tools via their APIs or exports. You keep using HubSpot, QuickBooks, Calendly, etc. exactly as before. The dashboard reads from them without changing how they work.

How long does it take to build?

+

A focused dashboard with 3-5 connected data sources typically launches in 2-3 weeks. More complex internal tools with custom logic can take 4-6 weeks depending on integration complexity.
