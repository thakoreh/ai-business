What an AI Opportunity Audit covers

The audit is a structured conversation about how your business actually runs day to day. We do not come in with a predetermined solution. Instead, we listen to where the friction is and look for the patterns that repeat.

Every audit produces three deliverables:

  • A workflow scan identifying the repetitive, time-consuming processes across your operations: lead follow-up, customer intake, appointment reminders, quote requests, reporting, document chasing, and more.
  • A ranked opportunity list with 3 concrete automation ideas, each scored by effort, risk, and estimated monthly hours saved.
  • A fixed-scope quick win recommendation: the smallest useful build that could go into production first, with a clear scope and timeline.

How the audit works

The process is designed to respect your time. It takes 20 minutes and requires zero technical preparation.

Step 1: We listen to your workflow

Tell us what a typical day looks like. Where does information get stuck? What tasks repeat every week? What falls through the cracks when someone is on vacation? We take notes and ask targeted follow-up questions.

Step 2: We map automation opportunities

Based on your workflow, we identify 3 specific processes where AI could save measurable time. For each, we estimate the hours saved per month, the complexity of building it, and the risk level.

Step 3: You get a scoped recommendation

We recommend the single highest-ROI automation to build first. If you want to proceed, we provide a fixed-scope proposal with timeline and cost. If not, you walk away with a clear map of your automation opportunities at no cost.

What makes this different from AI consulting

Most AI consultants deliver a slide deck with recommendations. The deck costs $5,000-$20,000 and ends up in a shared drive somewhere. The deliverable here is different: you get a scoped, buildable plan connected to a team that can actually ship it.

If the audit identifies a strong fit, the next step is a fixed-scope pilot build, not another strategy phase. Strategy is only useful when it leads to deployed software your team can test in the real workflow.

Who benefits most from an audit

The audit is most valuable for businesses that have:

  • 5-50 employees where manual processes are the bottleneck, not headcount
  • Repetitive admin work: lead intake, quoting, scheduling, reporting, follow-up
  • Customer-facing workflows where speed of response matters
  • Multiple tools (CRM, email, accounting, scheduling) that do not talk to each other
  • A team that wants AI but does not know where to start

If any of these sound like your business, the audit will give you clarity on exactly what to build first and why.

Common questions about AI strategy

Is the audit really free?

+

Yes. The 20-minute audit is free with no obligation. You get a workflow scan, 3 ranked opportunities, and a recommendation. If you want to proceed with a build, that is a separate scoped engagement.

Do I need to prepare anything for the audit?

+

No. Just be ready to talk about how your business runs day to day. If you have a list of tools you use (CRM, email, accounting, scheduling), that helps, but it is not required.

What if we already know what we want to automate?

+

Great. The audit still helps because we validate your assumption, estimate the actual ROI, and check for dependencies or risks you may not have considered. Often the "obvious" first automation is not the highest-ROI one.
